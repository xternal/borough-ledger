## Leads for the editor

The council's own spend files show work related to two Labour pledges. Spending that began before May 2026 is a continuing programme, not delivery of a new pledge, so these only become events on a card if a council paper links them to it.

- **AI-enabled CCTV** (lab-2026-ai-cctv): capital payments under "Capital - Public CCTV" and "Capital - Crime Fighting Strategy", £4.4m in the files since January 2024 (parking enforcement cameras left out). Look for a Cabinet decision on AI-enabled or weapon-detecting cameras.
- **£8m for playgrounds and parks** (lab-2026-parks): capital payments to 32 park and playground schemes (Frank Banfield Park, Bishops Park, Shepherds Bush Green, Ravenscourt Park and others), £2.0m in the files since January 2024. Look for the capital programme lines that make up the £8m.

## What the check covers

- The quote is copied exactly as printed, including the Conservative typo “dvelopments”.
- The page number is the PDF page.
- The status follows the same rule for both parties: the party with more than half the seats is the administration.
- Each card names its party and links its archived manifesto.

Not covered here: whether any other manifesto pledges should get cards (section 6 adds them in page order when an editor has capacity), and the history of free home care before 2026 (PRE_SHIP_REVIEW B3).
